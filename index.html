📖 Detailed Step-by-Step User Guide

Step 1: Paste Your Draft, Article, or Script Into the Editor · [Full-Width Text Editor (#textInput)]Paste or type your essay, documentation page, video script, or speech into `#textInput` (or pass `?text=...` / `?url=data:...` in the URL).✓ Result: All metrics compute instantaneously on every keystroke.
Step 2: Read Live Word Count, Reading Time & Flesch Grade · [Metric Cards Grid (#cards)]Review `#cards` for Words, Characters (with/without spaces), Sentences, Paragraphs, Reading Time (`225 wpm`), Speaking Time (`140 wpm`), Average Sentence Length, and Flesch Reading Ease (`0–100` + US Grade Level).✓ Result: Instant readability and length verification.
Step 3: Audit Top Keyword Density & Overused Terms · [Keyword Frequency Panel (#keywords)]Examine `#keywords` to see the top content words ranked by count and density percentage (with common English stop-words excluded).✓ Result: Spot repetitive phrasing or verify SEO topic coverage immediately.
Step 4: Balance Your Pacing with the Sentence-Rhythm Histogram · [Sentence-Rhythm Histogram (#rhythm)]Inspect `#rhythm`, which plots a bar for each sentence's word count so you can spot monotonous runs or overly dense 40-word sentences.✓ Result: Polished, well-paced prose with verified readability.

🎛️ UI Controls & Shortcuts Reference

  • Text Editor (`#textInput`) — Full-width live textarea that recalculates metrics on every keystroke.
  • 11 Stat Cards (`#cards`) — Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading/speaking time, and Flesch score.
  • Keyword Density (`#keywords`) — Stop-word-filtered top terms with occurrence counts and percentages.
  • Sentence Rhythm (`#rhythm`) — Visual bar chart of sentence lengths across your document.

💡 Pro Tips & Real-World Workflows

  • Aim for a **Flesch Reading Ease between 60 and 75** (8th–9th grade level) for technical blog posts and product landing pages so readers scan effortlessly.
  • Recording a YouTube Short or presentation? Check the **Speaking Time (`140 wpm`)** card to verify your script fits inside 60 seconds before recording.
  • AI agents can invoke this analyzer over MCP (`POST /tools/mcp` -> `text_stats`) or via URL deep-link (`textstats.html?text=...`).
